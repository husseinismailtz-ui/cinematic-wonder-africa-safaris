Before you travel

Plan the details with care.

Western and Eastern circuit logistics can vary by season and provider. Build flexibility into your schedule, especially around domestic flights, boat transfers, long road journeys, park access and weather-dependent activities.

May be included

  • English-speaking driver-guide or authorized local guide as specified.
  • Private safari transport, where specified.
  • Accommodation and meals listed in the confirmed itinerary.
  • Park fees and scheduled activities only when expressly included.
  • Airport transfers and drinking water where agreed.

Usually excluded unless agreed

  • International flights, visas and travel insurance.
  • Domestic flights and boat transfers not listed as included.
  • Tips, personal shopping and alcoholic beverages.
  • Optional excursions and additional accommodation.
  • Services or fees outside the confirmed quotation.
