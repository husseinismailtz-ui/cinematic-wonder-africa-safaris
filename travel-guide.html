01 · Every season has its own magic

Best time to visit Tanzania

The best time depends on what you would like to experience.

June–October · Dry season

  • Wildlife often gathers around rivers and water sources.
  • Vegetation is generally thinner, which can make animals easier to spot.
  • Conditions are often favorable for game drives in Serengeti, Tarangire and Ngorongoro.

January–March · Calving season

  • Wildebeest calving typically peaks around February in the southern Serengeti ecosystem.
  • Predator activity can be interesting to observe.
  • Green landscapes can offer rewarding photography opportunities.

April–May · Green season

  • The long rains generally occur during these months.
  • Lush landscapes, fewer visitors in some destinations and dramatic photography conditions.
  • Heavy rainfall may affect some roads and activities.

November–December · Short rains

  • Rain can bring renewed greenery and dramatic skies.
  • Birdwatching and wildlife viewing remain possible.
  • Rainfall patterns vary by region and year.
Travel tip: Wildlife movements depend on rainfall, vegetation and natural conditions. The Great Migration is continuous; specific sightings and river crossings cannot be guaranteed.
02 · Discover the wild heart of Africa

Tanzania's top safari destinations

Serengeti National Park

The land of endless plains. Famous for vast savannahs, wildlife and the Great Wildebeest Migration. Highlights include lions, elephants, giraffes, cheetahs, leopards and herds of wildebeest and zebras.

Ngorongoro Conservation Area

A natural wonder of Africa. The crater's remarkable landscape supports lions, elephants, buffaloes, hippos and endangered black rhinoceroses.

Tarangire National Park

The kingdom of elephants. Known for impressive elephant populations, ancient baobab trees and beautiful landscapes. The dry season can be rewarding as animals gather near the Tarangire River.

Lake Manyara National Park

A landscape of remarkable diversity. Explore groundwater forest, open grasslands and lakeside environments. The park is known for birdlife, elephants, hippos and occasional tree-climbing lion sightings.

Nyerere National Park

Explore Tanzania's southern wilderness. Discover vast landscapes, diverse wildlife and possible boat safari experiences on suitable routes and waterways.

Untamed beauty and wild landscapes. Celebrated for rugged scenery, wildlife and a sense of remoteness; a compelling destination for travelers seeking a quieter safari.

03 · Pack smart. Travel comfortably.

What to pack for your safari

Packing the right items can make your African adventure more comfortable. Consider your route, season, activities and any airline baggage limits.

Clothing essentials

  • Lightweight, breathable clothing.
  • Neutral-colored safari outfits.
  • Comfortable walking shoes.
  • Warm layer for early morning and evening game drives.
  • Sun hat, sunglasses and swimwear for Zanzibar or lodge pools.
  • Light rain jacket, especially during rainy months.

Safari essentials

  • Camera and spare batteries.
  • Binoculars for wildlife viewing.
  • Sunscreen and insect repellent.
  • Reusable water bottle.
  • Personal medications and a small daypack.
  • Power bank and charging cables.

Important documents

  • Valid passport and applicable visa or entry authorization.
  • Travel insurance details.
  • Flight information.
  • Accommodation and booking confirmations.
  • Any health documentation required for your journey.
Travel tip: Soft-sided luggage can be more practical for safari vehicles and small aircraft. Domestic flights may have strict baggage limits; check your allowance before departure.
04 · Prepare before you travel

Visa and entry requirements

Entry requirements depend on your nationality and may change. Some visitors need a visa, while others may qualify for visa-free entry.

  • Check visa requirements for your nationality.
  • Confirm your passport meets Tanzania's entry requirements.
  • Apply through official government channels where appropriate.
  • Keep accommodation and travel details accessible.
  • Check applicable vaccination or health documentation requirements before departure.
Important: Visa rules, fees and entry requirements can change. Confirm the latest information with the Tanzania Immigration Department before traveling.
05 · Travel prepared. Explore responsibly.

Health and safety

Your health and safety are important parts of a successful trip. This general guide is not medical advice.

Before your trip

Consult a qualified travel-health professional well before departure to discuss recommended vaccinations, malaria prevention and personal medical needs.

During your safari

  • Follow your professional guide's instructions.
  • Never approach or feed wild animals.
  • Stay in your safari vehicle unless your guide confirms it is safe to exit.
  • Drink safe water, protect yourself from the sun and use appropriate insect protection.
  • Respect national park regulations.

Travel insurance

Comprehensive travel insurance is strongly recommended. Consider medical emergencies, evacuation, trip interruptions and the activities in your itinerary.

06 · Understanding the seasons

Tanzania weather and climate

Tanzania's climate varies by region, altitude and time of year. Weather patterns are not identical every year; consider the specific destination and month when planning.

Northern safari circuit

Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire and Lake Manyara experience seasonal rainfall and temperature variations.

Mount Kilimanjaro

Temperatures change dramatically with altitude. Climbers should prepare for conditions from warm lower slopes to freezing temperatures near the summit.

Zanzibar and the coast

Coastal areas are generally warm and humid, making Zanzibar a popular beach destination.

07 · Managing your travel budget

Money, currency and payments

Tanzania's official currency is the Tanzanian Shilling (TZS). Some tourism businesses may also quote prices in US dollars.

  • Carry some Tanzanian Shillings for small purchases.
  • Use authorized currency exchange services.
  • Keep cash available when visiting remote areas.
  • Confirm accepted payment methods before traveling.
  • Inform your bank about international travel if necessary.

Credit and debit cards are accepted at many hotels and established tourism businesses, but availability varies.

09 · From the African wilderness to the Indian Ocean

Combine your safari with Zanzibar

After exploring Tanzania's national parks, continue to Zanzibar for white-sand beaches, turquoise waters, ocean sunsets and Swahili heritage.

  • Relax on the beaches of Nungwi, Kendwa, Paje or Jambiani.
  • Explore historic Stone Town.
  • Discover Zanzibar's spice heritage.
  • Enjoy a traditional dhow cruise.
  • Visit Jozani Forest.
  • Arrange snorkeling or diving excursions.
  • Discover local food, markets and coastal culture.

A safari and Zanzibar holiday offers a memorable combination of adventure, discovery and relaxation.

10 · Frequently asked questions

Tanzania travel FAQs

Is Tanzania good for first-time safari travelers?

Yes. Tanzania offers a wide range of destinations and travel experiences for first-time visitors, families, couples, photographers and experienced travelers.

How many days do I need for a Tanzania safari?

A safari can range from a short two- or three-day trip to an extended adventure. Five to seven days can be rewarding for several major Northern Tanzania destinations, depending on the route.

Can I combine a safari with Zanzibar?

Absolutely. Many visitors enjoy a wildlife safari followed by time relaxing on Zanzibar's beaches.

Is Tanzania suitable for family holidays?

Yes. Suitable itineraries depend on children's ages, accommodation policies, travel distances and planned activities.

Will I see the Big Five?

Tanzania is home to lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo and rhinoceros. Sightings are never guaranteed; some species, especially rhinoceroses and leopards, can be difficult to find.

What should I wear on safari?

Comfortable, lightweight clothing in neutral colors is generally recommended. Bring warm layers for early mornings and cooler highland destinations.

Do I need travel insurance?

Travel insurance is strongly recommended. Check that your policy covers planned activities, medical treatment and emergency evacuation.
