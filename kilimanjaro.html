Important climbing information

The summit is a goal.
Your safety comes first.

Kilimanjaro climbs involve significant altitude exposure and physical exertion. Prepare appropriately and seek advice from a healthcare professional familiar with high-altitude travel.

  • Obtain travel insurance covering trekking to your planned altitude and emergency evacuation.
  • Discuss fitness, health and altitude concerns with a qualified healthcare professional before travel.
  • Follow guide instructions, weather assessments and the agreed safety plan.
  • Be ready to turn back or descend if the guide considers conditions unsafe.
  • Summit success cannot be guaranteed and depends on many factors.
  • Confirm emergency response, evacuation arrangements and equipment in the booking agreement.

Plan your climb

Kilimanjaro through the seasons.

Mountain weather can change quickly. Confirm current conditions and route advice with your licensed operator before choosing dates.

January–March

Often suitable trekking conditions, though temperatures can be cold at higher elevations.

June–October

A popular climbing period that generally has relatively dry conditions.

April–May

Typically wetter, so some trails may be muddy and slippery.

November–December

Conditions vary and seasonal rainfall is possible.

Weather can change rapidly at any time of year. These are general seasonal patterns, not a forecast or guarantee of trail conditions.

Before you book

Understand your climbing quote.

Inclusions vary by route and operator. Ask for the confirmed written list before making arrangements.

May be included

  • Licensed mountain guides and support crew, as specified.
  • Kilimanjaro National Park fees.
  • Camping equipment or mountain hut accommodation, depending on route.
  • Meals during the climb and drinking water prepared according to operator procedures.
  • Transfers between hotel and mountain gates.
  • Mountain safety equipment and emergency arrangements specified in the agreement.

Usually not included

  • International flights and Tanzania visa fees.
  • Personal travel insurance and climbing clothing or equipment.
  • Guide and porter tips.
  • Alcoholic beverages and personal expenses.
  • Hotel accommodation before and after the climb unless specified.
  • Additional activities outside the confirmed itinerary.
