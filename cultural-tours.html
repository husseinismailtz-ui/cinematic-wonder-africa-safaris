Responsible cultural tourism

People are not attractions.

We aim to offer respectful cultural experiences that benefit local hosts and communities. Visits should be based on consent, fair arrangements and respect for local customs. Each host community decides what can be shared and when.

  • Ask permission before photographing people, homes or ceremonies.
  • Respect restrictions around sacred places and cultural practices.
  • Purchase crafts directly from participating artisans where possible.
  • Follow local guidance on appropriate clothing and behavior.
  • Arrange visits through local hosts or authorized providers.
  • Confirm access, travel times, availability and fees in advance.

Before you travel

Know what your quote includes.

Inclusions vary by route and provider. Confirm each item in your written quotation.

May be included

  • Professional local or cultural tour guide.
  • Private transfers as specified.
  • Accommodation and meals stated in the itinerary.
  • Guided cultural visits and community-hosted activities explicitly included in the quote.
  • Farm visits, demonstrations and airport transfers where arranged.

Usually not included unless agreed

  • International flights, domestic flights and ferry tickets.
  • Visa fees and travel insurance.
  • Personal shopping, souvenirs, tips and gratuities.
  • Alcoholic beverages and optional activities.
  • Additional accommodation, meals or community fees not expressly stated in the quote.
